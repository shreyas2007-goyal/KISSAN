Farmer Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective for all KisanSAATHI 2.0 users

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic operational information required to calculate net realization and deliver agricultural advisory: mobile number, name, district, state, landholding acreage, and uploaded crop photographs for AI screening.

2. How Agricultural Data is Used

Your crop data and price calculations are used exclusively to provide market recommendations and scheme eligibility filtering. We do NOT sell individual farmer crop records or financial numbers to third-party marketing brokers.

3. Web3 & Off-Chain Storage Policy

In strict compliance with Web3 security standards: No personally identifiable information (such as Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, or residential addresses) is ever recorded on public blockchains. Only cryptographic SHA-256 receipt hashes and transaction reference identifiers are anchored on-chain.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

Farmers and buyers retain full rights to request account deactivation and deletion of uploaded plant photographs at any time by contacting our support desk at privacy@kisansaathi.in.
