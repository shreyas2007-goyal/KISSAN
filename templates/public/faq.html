Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about KisanSAATHI 2.0 calculators, AI models, and Web3 verification.

1. How is Net Realisation different from standard Mandi price?

Mandi prices only display gross rates. The Net Realisation Calculator subtracts real-world transaction expenses: transport freight, labor charges (palledari), APMC user fees (1-2%), gunny bag packaging, storage, and moisture/foreign matter deductions. This reveals the actual cash you take home.

2. Can the AI Crop Wellness module diagnose all diseases with 100% certainty?

No. The AI Crop Wellness module provides preliminary digital screening based on computer vision patterns. It is not a certified laboratory diagnosis. The platform displays calibrated confidence scores and always recommends consulting your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) extension officer for critical crop distress.

3. What role does Web3 play in KisanSAATHI 2.0?

Web3 provides transparent, tamper-proof digital sale receipt verification. When a sale occurs, a canonical representation is created and its SHA-256 cryptographic hash is anchored on an EVM blockchain. Anyone with the receipt QR code can independently verify that the transaction terms have not been altered after settlement.

4. Does KisanSAATHI 2.0 require an internet connection for all features?

While live mandi price sync requires connectivity, previously cached prices and offline calculator estimations can be saved locally. The platform is optimized for low-bandwidth 2G/3G mobile networks.

5. How do FPOs benefit from KisanSAATHI 2.0?

FPOs can aggregate individual member harvest quantities into high-volume commercial lots. This gives the FPO collective bargaining power to demand premium rates from institutional buyers and mills, distributing the higher net realization back to members.
